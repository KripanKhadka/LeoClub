About Leo Club

Who We Are and What We Stand For

Our Story

Leo Club of Kathmandu Samakhusi is part of the largest youth-led organization in the world, operating under the umbrella of Lions Club International. Founded on the principles of service, leadership, and community engagement, we are committed to creating meaningful change in Kathmandu and beyond.

Our club brings together young professionals, students, and community leaders who share a passion for making a difference. Through various humanitarian projects, educational initiatives, and community programs, we strive to improve the quality of life for those in need.

  • Community Service: Engaging in meaningful projects that benefit our community
  • Leadership Development: Nurturing the next generation of leaders
  • Youth Empowerment: Creating opportunities for young people to grow
  • Global Network: Connected with thousands of Leo clubs worldwide
  • Cultural Exchange: Promoting understanding and friendship across cultures

Our Mission & Values

The Pillars of Our Organization

Service

We are dedicated to serving our community with compassion, integrity, and excellence in all we do.

Friendship

We build strong bonds of friendship and camaraderie among our members and with communities worldwide.

Leadership

We develop future leaders through mentorship, training, and real-world experience in service projects.

Unity

We work together as one global family, united in our commitment to making the world a better place.

Growth

We embrace continuous learning and personal development to become better versions of ourselves.

Excellence

We strive for excellence in everything we undertake, setting high standards for ourselves and others.

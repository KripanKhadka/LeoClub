Membership Requirements

To become an active member of Leo Club Kathmandu Samakhusi, you should:

  • ✓ Be between 12-30 years old (or 18+ to hold leadership positions)
  • ✓ Have a genuine interest in community service
  • ✓ Be willing to attend meetings and participate actively
  • ✓ Embrace the Leo motto: "Leadership, Experience, Opportunity"
  • ✓ Contribute membership fees (affordable and accessible)

Frequently Asked Questions

▶ What is the membership fee?

Our membership fees are kept affordable and accessible. Exact details can be discussed during your application meeting.

▶ How often do we meet?

We hold regular monthly meetings, plus additional events and projects throughout the year based on our calendar.

▶ Can I volunteer without being a member?

Absolutely! You can volunteer for specific projects without becoming a member. Join us for any event that interests you.

▶ Do I need previous experience?

No experience needed! We welcome enthusiastic individuals with all levels of experience and provide training and mentorship.

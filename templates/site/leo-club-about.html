About Leo Club of Kathmandu Samakhusi

Our Journey, Mission, and Impact

What is Leo?

Leo stands for "Leadership Experience Opportunity." Leo International is the youth organization of Lions Club International, serving approximately 200,000 members in 143 countries worldwide. Leo clubs empower young people to create positive change in their communities while developing valuable leadership skills.

Our Club History

Leo Club of Kathmandu Samakhusi was established to bring together young, dynamic individuals who share a passion for community service and personal development. Since our inception, we have been actively involved in various social, educational, and humanitarian projects that have positively impacted thousands of lives in the Samakhusi and greater Kathmandu region.

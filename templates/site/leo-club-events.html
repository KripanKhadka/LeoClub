Our Events & Activities

Join Us in Making a Difference

Monthly Event

Environmental Cleanup Drive

Every month, we organize community cleanup drives to beautify our neighborhood and promote environmental consciousness among residents.

When: Last Sunday of Every Month, 8:00 AM

Where: Samakhusi, Kathmandu

Duration: 3-4 hours

Quarterly

Educational Scholarship Program

We provide scholarships and mentoring to deserving students, helping bridge the educational gap and nurturing future leaders.

When: 4 times annually

Who: High school and college students

Value: Educational support & mentoring

Semi-Annual

Health Awareness Campaign

Free health checkups, medical consultations, and wellness programs promoting healthy living and disease prevention.

When: Twice yearly

Services: Health checkups, counseling

Open to: General public

As Needed

Disaster Relief Programs

When calamities strike, our club mobilizes resources and volunteers to provide immediate relief and support to affected communities.

When: Emergency basis

Activities: Relief distribution, rehabilitation

Impact: Community recovery & support

Monthly

Member Meetings & Socials

Regular meetings where we discuss initiatives, share experiences, and organize social events to strengthen our club community.

When: Every month

Where: Club headquarters

Open to: Members

Annual

Heritage & Cultural Preservation

We work to preserve and restore historical and cultural sites in Kathmandu, honoring our heritage for future generations.

When: Annually

Activities: Restoration, maintenance

Impact: Cultural preservation

Want to Join Us?

We're always looking for enthusiastic individuals who want to make a difference in our community. Whether you're interested in attending events, becoming a member, or volunteering for a specific project, we'd love to have you!
